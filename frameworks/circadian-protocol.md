# 🌅 Circadian Protocol (Huberman Lab + sleep science)

## Core principle
**Your brain runs on a 24-hour clock. Match the clock and discipline gets easier; fight the clock and discipline becomes impossible.**

## The non-negotiables

### Wake protocol (first 60 min)
1. **Same wake time every day** (±30 min, INCLUDING weekends)
2. **Sunlight in eyes within 30 min** (10 min outside, no sunglasses, no glass)
3. **No phone for 60 min** (cortisol spike + dopamine drain)
4. **Hydration before caffeine** (16-32oz water)
5. **Movement of any kind** (5 min walk minimum)

### Caffeine timing
- **First coffee: 90-120 min after waking** (not immediately — adenosine clearance happens naturally first)
- **Cutoff: 10+ hours before sleep** (caffeine has 5-6hr half-life; impacts sleep architecture even if you "fall asleep fine")

### Sleep protocol (last 60 min before bed)
1. **No screens** (or red-shifted only) for 60 min before sleep
2. **Cool room** (65-68°F / 18-20°C ideal)
3. **Same sleep time** (±30 min)
4. **Dark room** (any light = melatonin disruption)
5. **No food 2-3 hours before** (digestion disrupts sleep)

## Use this framework when diagnosis shows:
- Biological diagnosis: irregular sleep schedule, low energy, foggy mornings
- Biological diagnosis: phone-first morning, scrolling before bed
- Behavioral diagnosis: discipline crashes at predictable times of day

## Why this beats willpower
You can't will yourself disciplined when:
- Cortisol is spiking from morning phone use
- Dopamine baseline is crashed from late-night scrolling
- Sleep debt is making your prefrontal cortex literally underpowered

Fix the biology FIRST. Discipline becomes a downstream effect.

## Implementation in protocol

```markdown
## Circadian Anchors (DAILY, no exceptions)

MORNING:
- [ ] Wake by {TIME}, ±30 min including weekends
- [ ] Outside sunlight 10 min within 30 min of waking
- [ ] Phone in another room until {TIME, 60 min after wake}
- [ ] Coffee at {TIME, 90+ min after wake}

EVENING:
- [ ] Last caffeine by {TIME, 10+ hrs before sleep}
- [ ] No screens after {TIME, 60 min before sleep}
- [ ] In bed by {TIME}, ±30 min including weekends
- [ ] Room temperature, dark, phone outside bedroom
```

## Quick wins (impact ranked)

| Change | Effort | Impact within 2 weeks |
|---|---|---|
| Sunlight in first 30 min | Low | MASSIVE — circadian + mood + cortisol |
| Phone out of bedroom | Medium | MASSIVE — sleep quality + morning state |
| Same sleep time daily | Medium | LARGE — energy stability |
| Caffeine cutoff 10hr before bed | Low | MEDIUM — sleep architecture |
| Cool dark room | Low (one-time) | MEDIUM — deep sleep % |

## Anti-patterns
- "I just need less sleep" → false. ~99% of people need 7-9hrs.
- "Weekends I can sleep in" → no, this is "social jetlag," same as flying time zones
- "Caffeine doesn't affect my sleep" → the data disagrees, you're just used to bad sleep
- "I work better at night" → almost always cope for poor morning architecture
