# 🔄 CBT Reframes (Aaron Beck / David Burns)

## Core principle
**The thought you don't notice controls you. The thought you name and challenge loses power.**

CBT = catch the automatic thought → identify the distortion → write a balanced reframe → repeat until the new thought is automatic.

## The 10 most common cognitive distortions (Burns)

| # | Distortion | Example | Reframe |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | All-or-nothing | "If I can't do it perfectly, why bother" | "Done is better than perfect. 70% counts." |
| 2 | Overgeneralization | "I always fail at this" | "I failed last 3 times. The 4th is independent." |
| 3 | Mental filter | "Today was awful" (ignoring the wins) | "Today had X go well AND Y go badly. Both true." |
| 4 | Disqualifying positives | "Yeah I worked out, but only 20 min" | "20 min is more than 0. Vote cast." |
| 5 | Jumping to conclusions | "Everyone thinks I'm lazy" | "I have no evidence anyone is thinking about me." |
| 6 | Magnification | "If I miss one day the streak is ruined" | "Missing 1 day = missing 1 day. The streak resumes day 2." |
| 7 | Emotional reasoning | "I don't feel like it, so I can't" | "Feelings follow action, not the other way." |
| 8 | Should statements | "I should be more disciplined" | "I want to be more disciplined. Now what's the next step?" |
| 9 | Labeling | "I'm just lazy" | "I have lazy behaviors. I am not the behaviors." |
| 10 | Personalization | "If my friend is busy it must be me" | "People have lives. Don't assume it's about me." |

## The reframe protocol

```
1. Catch the automatic thought (write it down — exact words)
2. Name the distortion (which of the 10?)
3. Write the balanced version (what would a good friend tell you?)
4. Read the reframe out loud
5. Take action based on the reframe, not the original thought
```

## Use this framework when diagnosis shows:
- CBT diagnosis: dominant distortion is one of the 10
- CBT diagnosis: limiting identity label ("I'm just X")
- Performance diagnosis: failure-day script is missing or self-flagellating

## Implementation in protocol

```markdown
## Failure Recovery Script (use when you slip)

When my brain says: "{user's actual automatic thought from interview}"

I will catch it and reframe to: "{the balanced version}"

Then I will: {smallest possible behavior that gets the train moving again}

Example:
Brain: "I missed yesterday, the whole week is ruined"
Distortion: Magnification + All-or-nothing
Reframe: "Missing 1 day = missing 1 day. Today is independent."
Action: Do the smallest version of today's habit, right now.
```

## Anti-patterns
- Trying to "stop having the thought" — impossible, just CATCH and REFRAME
- Reframes that are too positive ("I'm amazing!") — they don't believe it, won't stick
- Doing this once and stopping — it's a daily practice for weeks before automatic
