# 🧪 Dopamine Balance (Anna Lembke / Andrew Huberman)

## Core principle
**Dopamine isn't the pleasure chemical. It's the WANTING/EFFORT chemical.** When baseline dopamine is high (from constant easy hits), effort feels impossible.

```
Easy dopamine sources:           Effortful dopamine sources:
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━       ━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Social media scroll              Hard exercise
Junk food                        Cold exposure  
Porn                             Deep work
Gambling / betting               Learning hard skills
Online shopping                  Creating something difficult
Doomscrolling news               Real conversation
Quick wins in games              Sleep + recovery
```

The first column **crashes baseline.** The second **rebuilds it** (and dopamine after effort feels GOOD, not depleting).

## The dopamine deficit cycle
1. Easy hit → dopamine spike → baseline drops below normal
2. To feel "normal," you need ANOTHER easy hit
3. Effort feels impossible because effort doesn't pay off vs the easy option
4. You label yourself "lazy" — but the brain is doing exactly what you trained it to do

## How to use it

### Phase 1: Detox (7-30 days)
Eliminate the highest-density easy dopamine sources. Pick 1-3:
- Phone first/last hour of day
- Social media apps deleted (web only)
- No porn / no gambling / no betting
- Notifications OFF on everything

**Withdrawal is real for 7-14 days.** Boredom returns. That's the system rebooting.

### Phase 2: Rebuild
Add effortful dopamine sources. Stack into morning:
- Cold shower (90 sec at the end)
- Sunlight within 30 min of waking (10 min)
- Movement (anything, 20 min)
- Hard cognitive task BEFORE phone touch

### Phase 3: Maintain
Easy dopamine becomes a tool, not a default. Phone for specific tasks. Social media in 20-min windows. The baseline holds because effort actually feels rewarding.

## Use this framework when diagnosis shows:
- Biological diagnosis: phone-first morning, doom scrolling, can't sit still
- Behavioral diagnosis: friction feels impossibly high for ANY effortful behavior
- CBT diagnosis: "I just don't feel motivated" pattern

## Implementation in protocol
```markdown
## Dopamine Hygiene (Phase 1: Days 1-14)
- [ ] Phone in another room overnight
- [ ] No phone first 60 minutes of day
- [ ] No phone last 60 minutes of day  
- [ ] {Social app} deleted from phone (web only)
- [ ] Sunlight within 30 min of waking, every day

## Dopamine Stacking (Days 15+)
- [ ] Cold shower (90 sec cold at end)
- [ ] Movement before noon
- [ ] One hard cognitive task before phone
```

## Key insights
- **Boredom is the doorway** — when you're bored and don't reach for the phone, baseline rebuilds
- **Withdrawal lies** — week 1 your brain SCREAMS that you NEED the dopamine. Ignore it. Week 3 you won't believe how clear you feel.
- **No partial credit** — "just sometimes" doesn't reset baseline. Need consistent days.

## Anti-pattern
- Trying to add discipline WHILE keeping the dopamine drain → impossible
- Reintroducing easy dopamine "just for weekends" → resets the cycle
- Skipping morning sunlight ("I'll do it tomorrow") → it's the foundation, not optional
